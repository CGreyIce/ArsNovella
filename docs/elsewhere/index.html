On a Monday in November 2026, a game called ELSEWHERE goes live. The pods cost two and a half thousand US dollars. Everything in its world can be sold for real money. You can stream it straight from your own eyes.

Most people are waiting to see if it’s worth it. Ice isn’t. For years he’s been the voice in his friends’ chats: raiding their streams, gifting their subs, keeping them company at three in the morning. On launch day he goes in first, with a sword, a shield, and twenty-one people watching.

Thirty-five friends are about to find out how it is. One by one, they’re going to follow.
