Thirty-six friends woke up in a white room at the foot of a tower with a hundred floors. Ten years later, one of them reached the top.

Ice killed the thing waiting on the hundredth floor with everyone he loved dead behind him, and the game gave him a choice: go home, or go back.

He went back. He’s twenty-six again, level one, with ten years of memories and a small fox-shaped system that remembers them too. In one minute, thirty-five friends will open their eyes. This time he’s already running, and this time, nobody dies.
