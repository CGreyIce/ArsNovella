Ice died in his sleep at twenty-six. He woke up in another world as Ars, a commoner’s son, and lived a whole life there: thirty-four years, a war, a daughter, and a legend he never asked for.

Now the Hero of the Redmarch has retired to a cabin on Lake Ilse, and all he wants is to fish.

Then the kingdom performs the Great Summoning, and thirty-five people stumble out of the light. He knows every one of them. On Earth, only three years have passed, and none of them have any idea that the quiet fisherman by the lake is the friend they lost.
